Our publishing program

Original books.
Recovered arguments.

Unruly Chain is an independent publishing imprint. We publish original books and Unruly Editions: significant public-domain works with new forewords, annotations, and afterwords.

The catalog is not organized around a single subject. It is organized around a kind of question: the ones that cut across disciplines and do not resolve into comfortable answers.

Our editorial position

Agreement is not a condition.

Unruly Chain seeks out alternative positions and is not afraid of anyone else’s ideas. We publish works worth examining, including works whose conclusions we dispute. We value negative results in scientific literature because knowing what failed helps knowledge advance. Agreement is not a condition of publication.

Some chains hold us back. This one pulls ideas forward.
