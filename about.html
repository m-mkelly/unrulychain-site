The imprint

Unruly Chain

Unruly Chain is the publishing imprint of M&M Kelly LLC. It is the home for Mitch Kelly’s books and related publishing projects.

Editorial posture

The imprint is built around books that make an argument, take intellectual risks, and remain readable without sanding away their point of view.

Production

The books are developed through MMK Writer, the production system used to manage source material, manuscript structure, review, revision, and publishing artifacts.
