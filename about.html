What it is

Unruly Chain is the publishing imprint of Mitch Kelly. It publishes books at the intersection of technology, organizations, and human capability.

The name carries useful ambiguity: a chain of causation that does not behave predictably, systems becoming emergent or escaping control, actors breaking established institutional chains. Independence from conventional authority. The consequences of capabilities escaping the institutions that historically controlled them.

Unruly Chain is not an AI publisher or a science publisher. Its scope is broader than any single subject. What the books share is a consistent intellectual posture: ideas and actors that do not remain obedient to the structures expected to contain them.
