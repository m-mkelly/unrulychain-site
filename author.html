Author

Mitch Kelly

Author of Centrifuge: Inside the Emerging AI Organization and the forthcoming novel Last Old Man.

Mitch Kelly is a career technologist, biomedical scientist, and strategic advisor. He holds a BS in Biology (Honors) and a BS in Chemistry from Florida International University, graduating Magna Cum Laude, and an MS in Bioinformatics from the University of Michigan, where he studied on a National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship. He is a third-degree black belt with 18 years of training, speaks three languages, and served in a U.S. Army Special Forces unit as a Russian linguist and intelligence analyst.

His career spans military intelligence, federal IT leadership, biomedical research, biotech executive leadership, and enterprise software strategy. He has led teams and built systems across nearly four decades of professional work, from classified intelligence operations to gene therapy development to organizational AI transformation.

Mitch writes about what happens when technology reveals the truth about organizations, institutions, and the people inside them. His work sits at the intersection of AI, organizational design, and human capability. Centrifuge examines how artificial intelligence exposes what organizations actually are. Last Old Man is a novel.

He is the founder of M&M Kelly LLC, an AI transformation advisory firm.
