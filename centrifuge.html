The book

Inside the emerging AI organization

Centrifuge is a practitioner’s examination of AI, organizational design, authority, trust, and human judgment. Its central argument is that AI accelerates the separation of genuine capability from performed competence.

The book follows what happens when organizations move past chatbots and begin to accumulate institutional cognition, delegate cognitive functions, and confront questions of sovereignty and dependence.

AI reveals what organizations actually are.
