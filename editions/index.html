Unruly Editions

Significant public-domain works, republished with new forewords, annotations, and afterwords. The historical author remains at the center of each Unruly Edition.

Unruly Chain seeks out alternative positions and is not afraid of anyone else’s ideas. We publish works worth examining, including works whose conclusions we dispute. We value negative results in scientific literature because knowing what failed helps knowledge advance. Agreement is not a condition of publication.
