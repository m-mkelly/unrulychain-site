For editors and authors

Unruly Chain publishes two kinds of books. The first is original books that ask difficult questions about technology, organizations, science, and human possibility. The second is Unruly Editions: significant public-domain works republished with a new foreword, annotations, and an afterword that place the historical argument inside a question people are arguing about now.

We are selective, and we invite most of our editors and authors. We also read proposals. Both kinds of book are publisher-funded: no editor or author ever pays us anything.

What we publish.

Our catalog is organized around questions, not subjects. We welcome proposals in these areas:

  • power, chokepoints, and routes to market;
  • science outside the institution, and bodily sovereignty;
  • longevity, aging, and the extension of human capacity;
  • cooperation, decentralization, and emergent order;
  • organizations, authority, and the design of work;
  • machines, minds, and human projection;
  • discovery and its consequences;
  • practice, the body, and measured wellbeing;
  • knowledge, expertise, and judgment;
  • anomalies, and how science handles them;
  • contemplative and esoteric traditions;
  • sexuality and sexual health;
  • the history and ethics of self-experimentation; and
  • money, cryptography, and decentralized finance.

We do not publish partisan or primarily political books.

What we do.

We select the project and carry the risk. We fund and handle design, production, ISBNs, distribution, the catalog, the press kit, and review copies. Every title gets a permanent page here, every author gets a page, and every release goes to our list. You do not need a website of your own.

What we ask.

Serious work. For an Unruly Edition, that means a substantial foreword, annotations, and an afterword, not a reprint with a short preface. For an original book, it means an argument worth disagreeing with. Agreement is not a condition of publication. We publish works we dispute.

How we assess.

Before we commit, we assess a sample of your work. The assessment has three parts: a structured audit of the reasoning, a descriptive profile of the writing, and an editorial letter from the publisher. It is free, it is never a condition of being published, and it is never sold.

Terms.

Editors of Unruly Editions receive the large majority of a title's net proceeds after a small production recoupment. Authors of original books receive 90 percent of net proceeds once production and a modest publishing fee have been recovered, all of it from sales and none of it from the author. Full terms come with any invitation or accepted proposal.

To propose a title.

Send a short note describing:

  • the work;
  • why it deserves a new edition or publication;
  • who it is for; and
  • how you would frame it.

Include a sample if you have one. Write to contact@unrulychain.com.
