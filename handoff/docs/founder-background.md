# Mitch Kelly: Background and Trajectory

## Professional Foundation

Mitch Kelly is a career technologist, biomedical scientist, and strategic advisor with nearly four decades of professional experience. He holds a BS in Biology (Honors) and a BS in Chemistry from Florida International University, graduating Magna Cum Laude, and an MS in Bioinformatics from the University of Michigan, where he studied on a National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship. He is a third-degree black belt with 18 years of training, speaks three languages, and served in a U.S. Army Special Forces unit as a Russian linguist and intelligence analyst.

His career spans military intelligence, federal IT leadership (site lead at FDIC headquarters), biomedical research, biotech executive leadership (CTO of BioViva Sciences, a gene therapy company working with mRNA and lipid nanoparticle platforms), and enterprise software strategy (Strategic Director at [RoleModel Software](https://www.rolemodelsoftware.com/)). A published profile in the International Business Times covers the [full career narrative](https://www.ibtimes.com/mitch-kelly-million-dollars-black-belt-refusal-flinch-3800212).

## M&M Kelly LLC

M&M Kelly is an AI transformation advisory firm founded by Mitch, with Amanda Kelly (his wife, an active American Airlines flight attendant) as co-founder and Creative Consultant. The firm provides readiness assessments, transformation roadmaps, fractional Chief AI Officer engagements, and practitioner training for organizations navigating AI adoption.

RoleModel Software is the current active client, an engagement that grew out of Mitch's time as their Strategic Director. This engagement provides stable income but operates in a sector facing serious structural headwinds from AI, and has a natural horizon. There is no second client. Revenue diversification is the immediate priority.

Over the past six to nine months, Mitch has been building professional presence on LinkedIn through original articles and analysis rather than actively selling. The strategy has been to establish credibility and a body of work that any future sales effort would build on. A podcast collaboration with a biopharma and life sciences content creator launched in July 2026. No outreach campaign, lead generation effort, or active sales motion has been attempted. The presence infrastructure exists. The demand generation mechanism does not.

## The AI + Health/Life Sciences Angle

Mitch's differentiation in the AI advisory space is the intersection of genuine technical depth and genuine scientific training. Most AI consultants are pure technologists. Most life sciences consultants are not technically fluent in AI. The combination of biomedical research credentials, biotech executive experience, military discipline, and nearly four decades of watching institutions succeed and fail from the inside is rare in any advisory market.

The natural niche for M&M Kelly's advisory practice is AI transformation for health, life sciences, and biotech companies. These organizations have massive data assets, are under board-level pressure to adopt AI, and face regulatory complexity (HIPAA, FDA, GxP) that generic AI consultants cannot navigate. The talent shortage in this specific intersection is well-documented. This is where Mitch's credentials create the most separation from the field.

## Structured Being

Structured Being is a separate venture from M&M Kelly. It is a health and wellness advisory company built around the premise that practices common across psychologically validated interventions and traditions throughout history are empirically real and should be part of daily life. The standard is not "functional." It is excellent.

Structured Being is not a software company. Consumer apps are passive income generators. The core business is health advisory services delivered at a premium, grounded in Mitch's biomedical science training and a proprietary methodology for identifying which practices actually produce measurable results, regardless of which tradition they come from or what explanation any tradition offers. The approach is empirical and pragmatic: if evidence shows a given practice produces a desired outcome, the practice is prescribed.

The brand is organized deliberately. "Structured Being" is the parent identity. Three philosophical pillars (body, mind, and spirit) frame the mission. Practice domains (each addressing a specific area of human performance and wellbeing) are how users interact with the brand. The naming convention is intentional: Structured Body, Structured Mind, Structured Spirit, and domain-specific practice brands. All corresponding domains are owned. The first practice domain is already in production with an app live and additional products in development.

**Structured Spirit** is the philosophical and doctrinal framework, built on Mitch's original work (the Convergent Practice Framework). It is the methodology that ties the health advisory practice together. Rather than accepting or rejecting any tradition's claims, Structured Spirit treats the convergent patterns across traditions as the signal worth investigating, then uses modern measurement and the scientific method to determine what actually works. This is not speculative philosophy. It is the operational framework behind real health advisory engagements where clients adopt specific practices and track measurable biological outcomes.

**Oracle** is the technology platform underlying Structured Being. It is a modular system where each wellness and measurement module plugs into a central architecture. Users choose which modules to subscribe to. A user with only a phone gets value from the core wellness features. As modules come online, each one expands what the platform can measure and prescribe. Oracle production begins Q4 2026.

The connection between M&M Kelly and Structured Being is deliberate. The AI advisory practice funds the development of Structured Being and builds the professional credibility (particularly in life sciences and health) that the wellness venture inherits. The same person who advises a biotech company on AI transformation is the same person building a platform that takes the empirical method seriously in domains where most practitioners rely on tradition alone.

## Publication and Thought Leadership

Mitch's ambitions extend into publishing and thought leadership as standalone activities. Some of these are adjacent to what a sales and marketing advisor would work on. Others require a different kind of partner, but are included here because they shape the full picture of where things are headed.

**Off the Natch** is a book project that documents an actual biomedical self-experiment Mitch conducted, drawing on his scientific training, his frustrations with institutional medicine and regulatory gatekeeping, and his conviction that the knowledge and technology to dramatically improve human health already exist but are locked behind institutional dysfunction and cultural timidity. The book recounts what happens when someone with the credentials to understand the risks decides to act on that knowledge personally. Nine years after the experiment, the results have been extremely positive, with one significant unexpected side effect. The writing voice is raw, direct, and intentionally provocative. For life sciences and biohacking audiences, this material positions Mitch as someone with unmatched firsthand credibility: a scientist who does not just advise but acts. The publication strategy (timing, attribution, audience targeting) is an open question with significant commercial potential.

**Cuba.** Mitch is documenting his father's life story through recorded oral history interviews. His father, who turns 90 in October 2026, was a government official in early Castro-era Cuba who was punished with forced labor for his Baptist faith and subsequently exiled to the United States. This is not a standard exile memoir. It involves a government insider destroyed not for political opposition but for religious conviction, a complex family history, and narrative threads connecting directly to documented events in Cuban history. Cuba is in active public conversation at a level not seen in decades. Comparable published works in this space have won the National Book Award. This is part of a broader literary arc Mitch envisions across multiple publications.

**Thought leadership and content monetization.** Beyond these specific projects, Mitch is actively interested in expanding his publishing footprint beyond LinkedIn. This includes potential Substack or newsletter offerings, additional long-form writing on AI and organizational leadership, speaking engagements, and establishing a presence in publications beyond the trade and social media space. The content pipeline already built on LinkedIn represents a substantial body of original work that has not yet been leveraged into paid channels or broader distribution.
