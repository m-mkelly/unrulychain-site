How unrulychain.com handles your information.

What this site collects

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, does not run analytics, does not collect personal data, and does not track visitors. No information is stored on our servers when you visit.

Newsletter

The newsletter subscription form on this site submits your email address directly to Substack (mitchkelly.substack.com). Your email and subscription are managed entirely by Substack under their own privacy policy. Unruly Chain does not store, process, or have independent access to email addresses submitted through the form beyond what Substack provides to publication owners.

You can review Substack's privacy policy at substack.com/privacy.

External links

This site links to external websites. Once you follow an external link, this privacy policy no longer applies. Each external site operates under its own policies.
